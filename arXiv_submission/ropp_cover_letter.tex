Dear Editors,

We are pleased to submit our manuscript entitled
\textbf{``Direct tests of conformal symmetry with Rydberg atoms:
assessment and proposals''} for consideration as a Research Paper in
\textit{Reports on Progress in Physics}.

\textbf{Justification Statement:} Conformal symmetry plays a central role in modern theoretical physics, particularly in understanding classical continuous phase transitions and quantum critical points. Recent advances in programmable quantum simulators based on Rydberg atoms offer an opportunity to test conformal symmetry directly by measuring equal-time two-point correlation functions in circular atomic arrays. For a 24-atom array, our simulations show that 2,000 measurement snapshots yield statistical errors below 2.5\%. We also develop optimized laser ramps that prepare the critical state with nearly 99.9\% fidelity in 1.2 microseconds at a favorable critical point, substantially reducing the evolution time compared with previous experiments. Such rapid preparation should mitigate decoherence, a major obstacle to experimentally realizing clean quantum critical states. Our work provides a practical route toward direct experimental tests of conformal symmetry and establishes a quantitative benchmark for high-fidelity quantum simulation of critical phenomena.

We believe that the combination of a fundamental question in quantum field
theory and a concrete experimental proposal makes this work particularly
suitable for the broad readership of \textit{Reports on Progress in Physics}.
We confirm that this work is original, has not been published previously, and is not under consideration for publication elsewhere. Both authors have approved the manuscript and its submission to Reports on Progress in Physics. The authors declare no conflicts of interest.

Thank you for your consideration.
